THE BRIEF

MakeMoneyDrop was conceived as a place to buy and sell gift cards, including cards someone received but would rather convert into cash. I designed the mobile experience and landing page, along with an admin dashboard concept for viewing platform activity.

DESIGNING FOR TRUST

A gift-card trade asks people to part with something valuable. Sellers need to understand how they will be paid; buyers need to know what they are purchasing. I treated clarity around the card, price, terms, and next step as part of the trust problem, rather than relying on a reassuring look alone.

TWO CLEAR PATHS

I organized the experience around buying or selling a card, so each person can get to the task they came for. In the purchase concept, card choice, amount, quantity, terms, and total price appear together before the final action. That lets a buyer check what they are getting before moving ahead.

A FAMILIAR ACCOUNT HOME

The mobile home screen puts the available balance and everyday actions up front: deposit, withdraw, buy or sell cards, send a card, and check rates. Transactions and saved cards have their own places in the navigation. I wanted the account to feel understandable even before someone explores every feature.

EXPLAINING THE PRODUCT

The landing page leads with the offer and shows the product screens before going deeper into its features. It also explores where trust signals would sit for a first-time visitor. Any social-proof figures in the mockup are concept copy, not verified project results.

PLATFORM VISIBILITY

The admin concept brings users, gift-card trades, trading volume, and recent activity into a single overview. Statuses such as completed, failed, and pending help a team spot what needs attention without opening every trade.
